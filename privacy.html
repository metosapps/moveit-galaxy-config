MoveIt Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Galaxy Store edition · Package com.moveit.app.gx

Your files stay on your device

MoveIt copies and moves files between internal storage, SD cards and USB drives. You choose the source and destination. The app verifies each copy at the selected verification level before it attempts to remove the original during a move. Your files, file names, paths, thumbnails and file hashes are not uploaded by MoveIt.

Local information and permissions

Transfer history, selected folder grants, preferences, automation rules and observed storage errors are stored locally. Android's folder picker grants access only to folders you select. Optional media access supports media categories. Notifications report transfer progress and storage events. A foreground service keeps user-started transfers running; boot events identify interrupted work for recovery. MoveIt does not request all-files access, location, contacts or access to your installed-app list.

Purchases

MoveIt Pro is a one-time lifetime item processed by Samsung Galaxy Store. MoveIt does not receive your payment-card details. Samsung's IAP service supplies product information and ownership records. The app sends the purchase ID to Samsung's HTTPS receipt-verification service, checks the app package and product, and stores the confirmed entitlement and purchase ID in private encrypted storage when encryption is available. New purchases and restoration require Samsung services and a network connection. A previously verified lifetime entitlement can work offline.

Remote settings and service providers

MoveIt may fetch a small configuration document from its dedicated GitHub Pages endpoint. It contains feature flags, release information, support links and advertising settings, not executable code. The hosting provider sees ordinary request information such as an IP address. No file information accompanies the request.

The app includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for optional advertising. Advertising starts disabled in this release preparation. If advertising is configured and enabled in a future rollout, it remains subject to consent, app activity and Pro ownership. Ad providers may process device identifiers, network information and ad interactions under their own privacy policies. Buying Pro removes ads. Ad privacy controls are in Settings.

Optional Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics adapters are present. Without an app-specific Firebase configuration they are inactive. If configured in a later release, optional usage events contain fixed event names and categorical counts; crash diagnostics may include device details and stack traces. You can disable usage analytics and crash reporting in Settings. We do not intentionally send file names, folder paths, file contents or purchase IDs through analytics.

Your controls

Settings allows you to revoke folder grants, disable optional analytics and crash reporting, manage ad consent, restore Pro and delete local MoveIt history/preferences. Deleting local app data does not cancel a Galaxy Store purchase or delete your personal files. Uninstalling removes app-private data; Android's own backup settings may preserve permitted preferences. Galaxy Store manages purchase records separately.

Children and changes

MoveIt is a general file-management tool and is not specifically directed at children. Policy changes will be reflected here and in the app's bundled policy when appropriate.
