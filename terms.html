Using MoveIt

MoveIt is a local file-transfer and storage-management tool. Use it only for files and devices you are entitled to manage. You control which folders, files and destinations you select.

Transfers and backups

Keep an independent backup of irreplaceable files. MoveIt copies first, verifies at your chosen level, and only then attempts source deletion for a move. Fast verification checks size, Standard adds sample reads and Strong performs complete checksums. Hardware failures, changes made by other apps and storage-provider behaviour can still affect a transfer. MoveIt cannot repair storage hardware, recover deleted files or move installed apps itself.

MoveIt Pro

Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock for the Galaxy Store app, without a subscription or recurring charge. Galaxy Store shows the applicable local price before payment and manages billing and refunds under its terms. Pro is tied to your Samsung purchase history; use Restore purchase with the same Samsung account. An internet connection and Samsung services are needed to purchase and restore. Previously confirmed lifetime access is cached for offline use.

Service availability and updates

Core file operations are designed to work offline. Optional ads, purchases, diagnostics and remote configuration depend on their service providers. Updates are delivered through Galaxy Store. Remote settings may disable an unavailable feature or request an update; they do not download or install executable app code.

Optional advertising and ad-free rewards

When advertising is enabled, MoveIt supports App Open ads during eligible app entry, interstitials at navigation breaks, native ads and optional rewarded ads. Watching a rewarded ad until the reward is earned pauses all advertising for 2 hours on this installation. The app privately stores the reward time and boot information so the timer survives reopening; it is excluded from backup. After expiry, eligible ads can return. This reward does not unlock Pro features. A verified MoveIt Pro purchase removes ads and unlocks all Pro features. Consent, availability, transfers and purchase flows can prevent an ad from showing.
