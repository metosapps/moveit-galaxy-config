MoveIt: SD Card & USB Transfer

Make room for what matters. Move or copy personal files between internal storage, an SD card and a USB OTG drive.

Copy. Verify. Then move.

Choose your folders, review your files and follow live progress. MoveIt verifies the destination before attempting to remove an original.

  • Manual file and folder transfers
  • Storage analysis and SD/USB tools
  • Saved transfer setups and automatic rules
  • Optional lifetime Pro, without a subscription

Available features depend on Android's folder permissions and your device's storage support. MoveIt does not move installed apps, repair SD cards or recover deleted files.
