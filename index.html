Can MoveIt move installed apps to an SD card?

MoveIt transfers personal files and folders. It does not move installed apps, repair SD cards, recover deleted files or increase RAM.

Will my SD card or USB drive work?

Your phone must support the connected storage. For USB drives, USB OTG support and a suitable adapter may be needed. Android controls which folders you can access. System and some application folders may be restricted, and read-only or full drives cannot accept files.

What happens if a transfer stops?

Reopen MoveIt to review an interrupted session. Recovery can resume the session, while incomplete individual files restart from the beginning. Check results before retrying, keep separate backups and do not disconnect storage during a transfer.

Does it work without the internet?

Core file transfers work offline. Purchases, purchase restoration, ads and optional online services need a connection. Previously verified lifetime Pro access can work offline.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. MoveIt Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase for the Galaxy Store edition. Galaxy Store displays the local price before checkout. To restore, sign into the same Samsung account and use Restore purchase in the app.

Which languages are available in the app?

The language picker offers 30 locales with partial interface translations. Onboarding, shared controls and the dashboard are translated; some feature screens remain in English. Full functionality may not be available on devices that do not support the app’s interface languages.

What does MoveIt do with my personal files?

You choose the folders MoveIt can use. MoveIt does not upload your file contents, file names, paths, thumbnails or file hashes. Optional service providers have their own data practices. Read the Privacy Policy for permissions, purchases, advertising and your controls.
