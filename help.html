MoveIt Help and Support

Move or copy files

Choose Move files or Copy files, select a source through Android's folder picker, select files, choose a destination and review the operation. A move removes each original only after its copy passes the verification mode you selected.

SD card and USB access

Connect your card or drive before choosing the destination. If Android revokes folder access, choose the folder again. Some system and application folders cannot be selected. A full or read-only drive cannot accept files. FAT32 has a per-file size limit. Many modern phones do not have an SD slot; USB OTG support depends on your device.

Interrupted transfers

Open MoveIt again to review an interrupted session. Recovery can resume the session; incomplete individual files restart from the beginning. Check the destination and retry failed files. Never disconnect a card or drive while a transfer is active.

Pro and restoration

Open MoveIt Pro to see the price returned by Samsung. If you already purchased Pro, sign into the same Samsung account in Galaxy Store and use Restore purchase. Check Galaxy Store purchase history before retrying an interrupted checkout.

Languages

The language picker offers 30 locales. Onboarding, shared controls and the dashboard are translated; some feature-specific text currently uses English.
