Need help moving a file, connecting storage or restoring Pro? Start with the steps below, or get in touch.

Talk to MoveIt support

chennoufo11@gmail.com

Include your MoveIt version, Android version, device model, storage type and any MI- error code. Do not send personal files, payment-card details or purchase IDs.

Move or copy your files

  1. Open Move files or Copy files.
  2. Select a source folder using Android’s folder picker and allow access.
  3. Choose your files and an accessible destination folder.
  4. Review the operation, start it and follow the progress.

Copy keeps your original files. Move removes an original only after its copy passes the verification method you selected. Fast checks size, Standard adds sample reads and Pro’s Strong verification checks the complete file by checksum. Keep independent backups of important files.

Connect an SD card or USB drive

Connect your card or drive before choosing the destination. Your phone must support the storage; a USB OTG adapter may be needed. If Android revokes access, select the folder again. Some system and app folders cannot be selected. A full or read-only drive cannot accept files. FAT32 has a per-file size limit.

The app can transfer personal files and folders. It does not move installed apps, repair cards, recover deleted files or increase RAM.

If a transfer is interrupted

Open MoveIt again to review an interrupted session. Recovery can resume the session; incomplete individual files restart from the beginning. Check the destination and per-file results before retrying. Do not disconnect storage during an active transfer.

Purchase or restore Pro

MoveIt Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock for this Galaxy Store edition. Open MoveIt Pro in the app to see the local price returned by Samsung before payment. Pro removes ads and unlocks full checksum verification, duplicate scanning, unlimited rules and saved setups, full history, CSV export and premium themes.

If you already purchased, sign into the same Samsung account in Galaxy Store and use Restore purchase. A connection and Samsung services are needed to purchase or restore. Previously verified lifetime access can work offline. If checkout is interrupted, check Galaxy Store purchase history before paying again. Galaxy Store manages billing and refunds under its terms.

Two hours without ads

When advertising is enabled, MoveIt can show App Open, interstitial and native ads, plus optional rewarded ads. Earn the rewarded-ad reward to pause all advertising for two hours on this installation. The timer survives reopening. After expiry, eligible ads can return. The reward does not unlock Pro; verified Pro ownership removes ads and unlocks all Pro features. Consent, ad availability, active transfers and purchase flows can prevent an ad from appearing.

Offline use and languages

Core file transfers work offline. Purchases, restoration, advertising and optional online services need a connection. The language picker offers 30 locales with partial translations. Onboarding, shared controls and the dashboard are translated; some feature-specific screens use English. Devices that do not support the app’s interface languages may not offer full functionality.

Privacy and permissions

MoveIt works with the folders you allow through Android’s folder picker. The app does not upload your file contents, names, paths, thumbnails or hashes. You can revoke folder grants and manage optional analytics, crash reporting and advertising consent in Settings. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for the full details.
